WORKFLOW

Build an AI research workflow

A practical recipe for ai research.

STEP 1

Define the question

Write a focused research question and list the decisions the research should support.

STEP 2

Collect sources

Gather primary sources and reputable secondary reporting; keep source URLs.

STEP 3

Summarize

Use an AI assistant to extract claims, evidence, dates and open questions.

STEP 4

Organize

Store findings in a document or database with citations and source notes.

STEP 5

Review

Fact-check important claims against the original sources before publishing or acting on them.
